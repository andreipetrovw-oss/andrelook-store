# Andrelook G1 search-intent map

Date: 2026-10-09  
Market: Tallinn / Estonia, with the existing approved European delivery service  
Languages: Estonian (ET), Russian (RU), English (EN)

## Method and limits

The map combines the production route inventory with current web-result samples in all three storefront languages. No paid keyword-volume account was available, so priorities below reflect business relevance, result-page fit and the ability of Andrelook to answer the intent with verified public facts—not asserted search volume. Search-result presence is a point-in-time signal, not a ranking promise.

The sample showed strong established competition from official brand sites and Estonian retailers for branded buying queries, while sizing queries are dominated by official brand guides and editorial sites. Andrelook was absent from the sampled results before G1. That makes precise brand/service pages and model-specific PDPs more defensible than broad generic “luxury” articles.

## Content decisions

- Created only two distinct, useful page types: localized Moncler and Parajumpers hubs. They aggregate the live catalog and explain the existing preorder/sizing service without claiming authorization or an official relationship.
- Did not create city permutations, generic luxury articles, inferred sizing advice, model guides that duplicate PDPs, or separate pages for every keyword variation.
- Existing category, preorder, how-to-order, delivery/payment, FAQ, About and PDP content already satisfies the remaining legitimate intents.
- Exact PDPs remain canonical for model, price, availability, colour, image and size-chart queries. Brand hubs remain canonical for brand + Andrelook/Tallinn/Estonia discovery.

## Follow-up measurement

After Search Console and Bing verification, review real query impressions rather than expanding content from intuition. A new page should be considered only when query data shows a distinct unmet intent and Andrelook has enough verified public information to answer it better than an existing route.
