# Minimal consumer protection — 28 October 2026

This note replaces the earlier manual-template/PDF proposal. Baseline:
`76014c620f6a4b9ecfb87f1eb6a7bc8e6adac27c`.

## Existing sales process

Selection → inquiry → personal agreement/payment → existing CRM/private tracking
→ delivery → verified review. No extra pipeline, checkout, mandatory checkbox,
manual contract, repeated chat script, PDF or acceptance screen is introduced.
An inquiry and its contact consent are **not final purchase acceptance**.

Final terms and the customer's agreement must be retained from the existing
personal communication. Provide the complete agreed details and applicable terms
once, before payment; retain the actual agreement unchanged and accessible to the
customer. Existing email can provide a durable copy where used. Do not label the
current owner-only new-inquiry notification a customer contract confirmation.

**Unresolved confirmation limitation:** current code neither delivers a final
contract confirmation to every customer nor records their offsite acceptance.
A CRM status click is not customer acceptance. A normal editable website link
alone is not a durable copy. No automatic confirmation can truthfully supply
undefined seller details or infer acceptance from payment. The smallest necessary
operational safeguard is retaining the complete existing agreement/terms in a
durable channel; where that channel cannot do so, use an existing email channel
for that one confirmation rather than creating another sales system. No new
mandatory messaging ritual or automated customer send was added.

## Withdrawal

Reuse the old legal branch's independent WithdrawalRequest store and Resend
infrastructure, not its unrelated migration/order fields/catalog corrections.
The customer opens the localized function, supplies name, purchase details and
acknowledgement email, then explicitly confirms. No account, matched order,
reason or owner approval is required. The record and original statement/timestamp
are immutable; retries with the same key cannot create another declaration or
change its contents. Successful email acknowledgements are not resent. Failures
remain recorded and the customer can retry delivery using the same declaration.
Preview notification recipients are restricted to the existing business inbox.

Prominent existing-page links and the footer expose the function in ET/RU/EN.
It does not cancel an order, alter a payment, refund money or change logistics.
No new CRM screen or unrelated infrastructure. The only migration creates/reuses
the previously designed standalone declaration table. No existing order is updated.

## Lawful seller protections

Mandatory withdrawal remains where applicable. No extra voluntary period,
automatic goodwill exchange, fixed deduction or restocking fee. Direct return
shipping is the buyer's only when lawfully disclosed before contract and no
contrary agreement/legal duty applies. Excess wear/damage requires actual
documented evidence and a lawful diminished-value assessment. Defect remedies
and delivery-failure rights remain separate. Previously concluded contracts are
not changed by the new voluntary terms.

[VÕS §56⁴ / national commencement 1 September 2026](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/et/akt/127052026002)
covers online-interface contracts; offsite messaging is not assumed exempt merely
because the website accepts inquiries rather than card payments. The function is
provided without trying to exclude such customers.
[Current VÕS](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/et/akt/V%C3%95S) and
[TTJA](https://ttja.ee/en/business-client/entrepreneurship/consumer-protection-requirements/e-commerce)
support the mandatory withdrawal/return-cost/diminished-value rules.
Do not copy obsolete ODR-platform references from older guidance.

Seller identity, geographical/return addresses and verified supplier information
remain **deferred owner-dependent facts**. They have not been invented and are not
requested again here. Complete legal compliance cannot be declared without the
mandatory information. No changes to historical orders, financial records,
tracking credentials, reviews, product facts or analytics.
