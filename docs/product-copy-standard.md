# Andrelook product-copy foundation

Status: content structure and owner workflow only. No catalog copy is generated or approved in Phase 6D.1.

## Customer-facing structure

Each product keeps independent RU, ET and EN records. The CRM presents one language at a time while preserving the three stored translations.

Supported content may include:

1. confirmed model/product identity;
2. confirmed category;
3. a concise visual description based on visible evidence;
4. material or construction facts only when directly supported;
5. fit or sizing guidance only when verified by source evidence;
6. owner-approved sizes and colours;
7. owner-approved availability;
8. owner-approved preorder or delivery explanation.

The preferred tone is concise, premium and practical. Avoid generic SEO filler and avoid repeating the product name merely to increase keyword density.

## Prohibited unsupported claims

Never invent material, manufacture country, authenticity, performance, stock, warranty, GTIN, reviews, ratings or delivery promises. Missing evidence remains visibly unresolved in the CRM.

## Future assisted translation boundary

The current data model already separates ProductTranslation by locale. A future translation assistant may propose text, but it must not write directly to approved storefront content or infer missing product facts. Every proposed translation must remain reviewable against the source-supported RU record and require an owner save/approval action.
